Dungeon Conquest Preview

English translation of the Korean LaTale update notice describing the upcoming Dungeon Conquest system.

Dungeon Conquest System Added

  • This system grants stats to characters on your account when a dungeon is cleared.
  • In the Dungeon Information UI, you can check each dungeon's Dungeon Conquest effects and the total options you have obtained.
  • The Dungeon Conquest filter lets you view conquered and unconquered dungeons.
  • When the June 24 update was applied, conquest status was determined based on whether each dungeon reward had already been claimed in the Guidebook.

Stats and effects transferred to Dungeon Conquest

  • Item Codex stats were transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed from the Item Codex.
  • Engraving stats were transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed.
  • Item Drop Rate stats on combat equipment were transferred to Dungeon Conquest.
  • Ely Gain stats on some equipment were transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed. For equipment whose Ely Gain enchantment was removed, one of the remaining options can be selected for an additional enchantment.
  • Effects from some food items were transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed.
  • Effects from some Title Codex entries were transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed.
  • Effects from some content skills were transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed.
  • Effects from character Trait skills were transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed.
  • The Item Drop Rate option on Kang Tae-gong equipment was transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed.
  • The effect of the Quest Reward UP skill was transferred to Dungeon Conquest and removed.
  • Dungeon Conquest effects through the Amaranth Nova dungeon were set to compensate for stats affected by content simplification. Please note that dungeons added afterward may provide lower stats than earlier dungeons.
This is a translation of the Korean update notice. Final English terminology may differ when the feature is officially localized.
